## Description
Ifeoma, a fictional freelance subtitler in Enugu, cannot pay for a captioning tool with her card. Meruit charges for subtitles on Solana only for the minutes delivered, through an x402 `upto` payment on the Solana payment-channels program, and returns the unused ceiling in the same settlement. She tops up from the chain she already holds through Aurora Intents, and can take her money back from her wallet alone.
