            when her naira card is declined for international web payments and the job pays $35.
ACTION:     She tops up once from Tron USDT through an address she keeps, approves one ceiling per job in her
            wallet, watches captions appear over her video, downloads the .srt, and is charged on Solana only
            for the minutes delivered; the rest returns in the same settlement.
DISCOVERY:  everyone assumes pay-per-use online needs a card or a subscription; in reality a Solana payment
            channel charges exactly what was delivered, from money that arrived from any chain.
PROOF:      generated into docs/FACTS.json from real runs: jobs, overcharges (target 0), money returned, forced
            exits, deposit arrival minutes, cost per transcribed minute. Misses are named. No run is recorded yet.

Primary guarantee: you pay only for what you received, and you can always get the rest of your money back, even if Meruit disappears. Both halves are enforced by the program (the deposit is the ceiling, the refund is bound to the payer), not by Meruit's server.
