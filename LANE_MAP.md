# LANE_MAP.md

Neutral description of the lanes around this build. No names.

| Lane | What it tends to do well | What it tends to skip | Where Meruit stands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent spending controls | clear limits as the product | people as the buyer | a person is the buyer, with one ceiling per job |
| Wallet and trading tools | simulate before sign, plain safety notes | per-use billing, any-chain funding | a consumer payment flow with a buyer-run exit |
| Governance and treasury | receipts that tie decision to transaction | everyday consumer use | one receipt per job, with signatures |
| Consumer apps with a founder story | personal opening, demo without a wallet | proof behind claims | measured numbers only, with misses published |
